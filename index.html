Fight the Urge

A free web-based alternative to I Am Sober, created specifically for people who self-harm.

Track your progress, get through difficult moments, and find coping strategies when you need them.

How it works

01 — Start your timer

Choose when you want your current streak to begin and watch your progress update automatically.

02 — Fight the urge

When an urge hits, find coping strategies, grounding exercises, distractions, and other ways to get through the moment.

03 — Keep track

Your account keeps your current streak and previous streaks so you can see your progress over time.

Free. Private. No judgment.
